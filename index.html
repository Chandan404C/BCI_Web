About Build Const Industries & Agro Pvt Ltd

Building Progress. Growing Prosperity.

BCI Construction Professional construction and RCC solutions for residential, commercial, and structural projects. Our services include RCC foundation and wall construction, concrete and reinforcement work, building construction, boundary walls, finishing, renovation, and improvement works.

BCI Agriculture & Trading Reliable sourcing, trading, and bulk supply of quality agricultural products for wholesalers, distributors, retailers, and institutional buyers. Our product range includes rice, grains, pulses, seeds, maize, oilseeds, spices, feed products, and other agro commodities.

Company Trading Process

From Requirement to Reliable Delivery

Our streamlined approach connects buyer requirements with reliable sourcing, clear commercial terms,
quality verification, and coordinated logistics,
ensuring a smooth and dependable supply process from enquiry to delivery.

1

Requirement & Sourcing

Understand buyer requirements and identify suitable suppliers based on product, quantity, grade, packing, and destination.

2

Quotation & Quality Check

Compare commercial terms and review samples or product specifications to ensure quality and suitability.

3

Order & Logistics

Confirm the purchase order and coordinate procurement, dispatch, transportation, and other logistics.

4

Delivery & Completion

Ensure the products are delivered safely and efficiently according to the agreed terms and requirements.
