About Build Const Industries & Agro Pvt Ltd

Build Const Industries & Agro Pvt Ltd (BCI PVT LTD)

is a diversified business focused on construction, RCC work, agricultural product trading, sourcing and bulk supply. Our construction division provides solutions for residential and commercial building requirements, including RCC walls, structural work, concrete work and building construction. Our agriculture division focuses on sourcing and trading rice, grains, pulses, seeds, feed, maize, oilseeds, spices and other agricultural commodities.
